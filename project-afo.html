Ankle-Foot Orthosis (AFO) Design for PAD

Innovative gait assistance for patients with Peripheral Artery Disease.

Project Overview

This project involved the design and development of a customized Ankle-Foot Orthosis (AFO) specifically tailored for individuals suffering from Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD). The goal was to improve gait stability and reduce metabolic cost during walking, addressing the unique biomechanical challenges faced by this patient population.

Technical Methodology

  • Musculoskeletal Modeling: Utilized OpenSim to simulate patient gait and identify critical stress points during the stance phase.
  • CAD Design: Developed a parametric 3D model in SolidWorks, ensuring compliance with ISO 22523 standards for external limb prostheses and orthoses.
  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA): Conducted stress and strain simulations to optimize the thickness-to-strength ratio of the carbon-fiber reinforced composite.
  • Material Selection: Selected biocompatible polymers and lightweight composites to ensure durability and comfort for daily use.

Key Results & Impact

15% Reduction in Gait Deviation
ISO 22523 Full Standard Compliance
20% Weight Optimization

The final design demonstrated a significant improvement in ankle stability and was validated through simulated testing. This project showcased the integration of biomechanical modeling with advanced manufacturing techniques to solve real-world clinical problems.
